Earthquake in Chocó

The Colombian Geological Service (SGC) reported a new earthquake this Saturday, September 12, 2026, in the municipality of Sipí, in the department of Chocó.

More information of the earthquake

According to Updated Bulletin 1, this earthquake occurred at 9:07 p.m. local time, with a magnitude of 3.2 and a depth of 40 kilometers.

Coordinates

The event's coordinates were set at a latitude of 4.56 and a longitude of -76.68; this data allows us to determine the earthquake's location and track it within this region of western Colombia.

The official agency noted that this information is subject to change, As the behavior of this earthquake is analyzed, new data regarding the event will be obtained.

This report is part of the ongoing monitoring of seismic activity within the national territory conducted by the SGC.
This is done to keep the Colombian population informed and to provide information for risk monitoring and management.
