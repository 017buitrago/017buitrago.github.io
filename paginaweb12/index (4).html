How to Embed a YouTube Video in a Website

This tutorial shows you, step by step, how to put a YouTube video on your own web page.

Step 1: Find your video on YouTube

Go to youtube.com and open the video you want to show on your website. Make sure the video does not have any mature content or profanity.

Step 2: Click the Share button

Below the video, click the Share button. A window with sharing options will open.

Step 3: Choose Embed

In the sharing window, click the Embed option. If you do not see it, the person who uploaded the video may have turned off embedding. In that case, pick a different video.

Step 4: Copy the embed code

A box will show a line of code that starts with <iframe>. Click the Copy button to copy the code.

Step 5: Paste the code into your HTML file

Open your HTML file in your code editor. Paste the code inside the <body> tag, in the place where you want the video to appear.

Step 6: Save the file and test it

Save your HTML file and open it in your browser. You should see the video player on your page. Click play to make sure the video works.
